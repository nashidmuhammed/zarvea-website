01 • About Zarvea

The Essence of Nature — Rooted in Kerala

Rooted in the fertile soils and centuries-old agricultural legacy of Kozhikode and the Western Ghats, where the world's most prized spices have flourished for generations.

Generations of Spice Mastery

Based in Kozhikode, Kerala, we specialize in farming, sourcing, processing, and supplying carefully selected natural spices, including green cardamom, black pepper, cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, and turmeric, along with selected tea, coffee, and cold-processed coconut products.

Our philosophy is simple — to preserve the true essence of nature in every product we offer. From responsible farming and ethical sourcing to careful handling, quality-focused processing, and hygienic packaging, we are committed to delivering products with exceptional purity, freshness, aroma, and authentic flavor.

We cater with distinction to wholesale distributors, luxury food manufacturers, hotels, restaurants, and international importers looking for dependable quality and consistency.

02 • Terroir & Provenance

Exceptional Spices Begin With Exceptional Origins

We believe that authentic aroma, rich essential oil concentration, and medicinal vibrancy are created by the micro-climate, soil elevation, and traditional farming stewardship of Kerala.

Own Plantations & Trusted Smallholders

Vandanmedu & Cardamom Hills, Idukki

Elevation: 900m – 1,400m MSL

Idukki's high-altitude tropical cloud forests and volcanic loamy soil provide the most celebrated terroir for green cardamom, true cinnamon quills, whole cloves, nutmeg, and high-curcumin turmeric. Our own plantation in Vandanmedu benefits from continuous mist, dense shade tree biodiversity, and seasonal monsoon rains, yielding cardamom pods with unparalleled plumpness and aromatic volatile oils.

Cardamom Own Plantation
Cinnamon Selected Farmers
Cloves & Nutmeg Local Heritage Trees
Turmeric High Curcumin
Own Plantation & Tribal Grower Partners

Pulpalli, Wayanad High Highlands

Elevation: 700m – 1,100m MSL

Wayanad is the birthplace of historical Malabar Black Pepper. Cultivated in our dedicated plantation at Pulpalli, our pepper vines climb native forest silver oaks. The cool highland climate allows the berries to mature slowly, concentrating piperine, essential resins, and a penetrating, woody pungency that has made Malabar pepper legendary across world spice markets for two millennia.

Black Pepper Pulpalli Plantation
Robusta Coffee Shade-Grown Beans

Palakkad Slopes & Nilgiri Borders

Elevation: 500m – 950m MSL

Bridging the central Kerala plains with the Western Ghats mountain pass, Palakkad features micro-climates ideal for slow-grown tea bushes and select aromatic varieties. We work with trusted multi-generational farmers who practice low-intervention organic composting and hand-selective plucking.

Tea Leaves Whole Orthodox

Dedicated Island Mill & Cold Processing

Lakshadweep Archipelago

Pristine Coral Atoll Biosphere

The Lakshadweep islands boast one of the world's most pristine coconut ecosystems. Grown in mineral-rich coral sand completely free from mainland pollutants and chemical pesticides, these micro-coconuts produce oil exceptionally rich in lauric acid. We process raw materials directly in our own mill in Lakshadweep, cold-extracting Virgin Coconut Oil without heat or chemicals.
